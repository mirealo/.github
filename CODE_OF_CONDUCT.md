# Code of conduct

## Our standard

Mirealo is committed to professional, respectful, and evidence-based
collaboration. Be constructive, respect privacy, communicate in good faith, and
focus criticism on ideas, behavior, and outcomes rather than people.

## Unacceptable behavior

Unacceptable behavior includes:

- harassment, discrimination, threats, intimidation, or sustained hostility;
- sexualized conduct or unwelcome attention;
- publishing another person's private information;
- deliberate disruption, spam, impersonation, or manipulation;
- retaliation against a person who raises a concern in good faith;
- using technical disagreement to justify personal attacks.

## Reporting concerns

Do not publish a conduct report or sensitive personal information in an issue.
Report concerns privately to
[conduct@mirealo.com](mailto:conduct@mirealo.com). The current sole maintainer
monitors this inbox. Include only the context needed to understand the concern,
the affected space, and any immediate safety risk; do not send credentials or
unrelated private material.

The maintainer will make a reasonable effort to acknowledge receipt and
communicate the next practical step or follow-up when circumstances allow.
Availability, safety, platform processes, and the complexity of the report vary,
so this policy does not promise a response or resolution deadline.

Conduct reports are handled separately from vulnerability reports. Send
suspected vulnerabilities through the private process in
[SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md), not to the conduct inbox. If one event raises both
conduct and security concerns, keep exploit details in the vulnerability report
and use the conduct inbox only to identify the related private report.

### Reports involving the sole maintainer

Mirealo currently has no second maintainer who can provide independent internal
review. A report involving the sole maintainer may still be sent to the conduct
inbox, but that route is not independent and must not be represented as such.

For abusive content hosted on GitHub, use GitHub's
[Report content](https://support.github.com/contact/report-abuse) process. For
conduct on another service, use that service's reporting or moderation process.
Contact appropriate local emergency or legal services for an immediate threat
or a matter requiring authority Mirealo does not have. These are external routes,
not a Mirealo appeal or a promise of action by another organization.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove content, issue a warning, limit participation,
close discussions, or temporarily or permanently restrict access. Responses
will be proportionate to severity, impact, history, and risk to the community.

Report content and the identities of involved people will be limited to what is
reasonably needed to assess or act on the concern. Absolute confidentiality
cannot be guaranteed when safety, law, platform enforcement, or necessary
external escalation requires disclosure.

## Scope

This policy applies to Mirealo-managed repository spaces and to public
representation of a Mirealo project. It does not replace GitHub's Terms of
Service, Acceptable Use Policies, or reporting mechanisms.
